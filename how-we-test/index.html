How we test

Every tool is tested on a brand new free account, on real tasks, by a human. We record free tier limits, whether a card is required, export quality and speed.

Scoring

Output quality 40%, free tier generosity 30%, ease of use 20%, privacy 10%.

Rechecks

We recheck every listing at least monthly and stamp the date on each card.

Independence

Advertisers and affiliate partners cannot buy placement or scores.

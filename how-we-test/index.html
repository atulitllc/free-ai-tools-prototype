How we test

Every tool is tested on a brand new free account, on real tasks, by a human. We record the free limit, watermark, signup, on-device vs upload, commercial use terms, and capture a dated screenshot of the pricing page.

Ranking

Output quality, free tier generosity, ease of use and privacy. Never by affiliate payout.

Rechecks

Monthly at least. If a free plan disappears we mark it 'No longer free since' with the date.
